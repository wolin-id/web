ABOUT THE COMPANY · 기업 소개

엔트로피패러독스 주식회사

Entropy Paradox, Inc.

Based in Seoul, Entropy Paradox, Inc. designs enduring digital identity and personal data infrastructure. We build Wolin (月印千江) so individuals, creators, and AI companions can move freely across open worlds without surrendering ownership of their lives to platform silos.

01 · MISSION & DOCTRINE

Ordering Digital Chaos Around Human Dignity

Applications appear and vanish faster every year, yet a person’s memories, creative works, and relationships deserve a quiet, permanent home.

01

Human Ownership First

Every user holds their own Digital Passport and personal Pod. We never sell personal profiles, and anyone can export their entire Pod at any time without lock-in.

02

Open Web, Zero Speculation

We build on open W3C web standards initiated by Sir Tim Berners-Lee — delivering true digital ownership with zero speculative coins, tokens, or gas fees.

03

Craft That Endures

Rather than chasing noisy hype cycles, we engineer calm, reliable software systems where every creator world and user Pod remains resilient for decades.
