Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 · wolin.id

1. Zero-PII & Passwordless Architecture

Wolin does not store user passwords. Authentication relies on verified OAuth2 providers (Google, Apple, Kakao, Naver, GitHub, Discord), email magic links, or Passkeys. We collect only the minimum information required to operate your account: your verified email address, Pod identifier, and linked provider IDs.

2. Payment Processing

Payment card details are processed directly by our PCI-DSS compliant payment partner (Airwallex) and are never stored on Entropy Paradox, Inc. servers.

3. Pod Data & Access Control

Your Pod data is governed by your explicit Web Access Control (WAC/ACP) rules. Third-party apps only receive the specific scopes you approve in the Consent UI, and you may revoke access at any time.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You may request account deletion at any time by contacting hello@wolin.id. Upon deletion, your Pod contents and personal email records are purged, while the Pod handle is tombstoned to protect against impersonation.
